You filmed it in portrait. You need it in landscape. WideIQ turns a vertical clip into a real widescreen video — right on your iPhone, in a couple of taps.

100% on-device. WideIQ has no account, no server, and no upload step. Your video is converted on your iPhone and saved back to your Photos library. It never leaves your phone.

What it does

  • Pick any portrait video from your library and convert it to true landscape.
  • Two styles: Blur fill keeps the whole frame and fills the sides with a soft blurred backdrop; Crop fill zooms in for an edge-to-edge widescreen picture.
  • Real output quality: choose 1080p (1920 x 1080) or 4K (3840 x 2160) H.264 — a proper video file, not a screenshot trick.
  • Preview, save, share. Watch the result full screen, save it to Photos, or send it anywhere with the share sheet.

Why it exists

Phones film tall; screens, TVs, projectors, and most editing timelines want wide. Uploading a private clip to some website to fix that is a bad trade. WideIQ does the conversion locally with Apple's own media frameworks, so it is fast, it works in airplane mode, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.
