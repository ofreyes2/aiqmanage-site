Common questions

How do I get updates?

Apps published on the App Store update through the App Store like any other app (turn on automatic updates in iOS Settings if you want them hands-free). Apps in early access update through Apple's TestFlight app — TestFlight notifies you when a new build is available.

Where is my data stored?

For our on-device apps — including GarageIQ, WideIQ, NightIQ Sleep, and VaultIQ's records — your data lives only on your own iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers; nothing is uploaded. Deleting an app deletes its data. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@aiqmanage.com with the app name, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened versus what you expected. If the app is on TestFlight, you can also send feedback straight from TestFlight (screenshot, then tap Share Beta Feedback).

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple as well: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. Apple usually answers within a day or two.

How do I delete my data?

Because data is stored on your device, you are always in control: delete individual entries inside the app where offered (for example, single nights in NightIQ Sleep), or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Can I suggest a feature?

Please do. Most of what these apps do well started as a request. Email us with the app name and the problem you are trying to solve.
