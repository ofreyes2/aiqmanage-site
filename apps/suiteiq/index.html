What it does

SuiteIQ reads the public booking feeds of Four Seasons, Waldorf Astoria, Conrad and LXR for 13 months of nightly rates and every room type, plus eleven cruise lines from Seabourn to Virgin Voyages with per-person fares by sailing. Filter by state, brand, month, length or price, tap two calendar days to total a stay, and send a quote request to a Preferred Partner or Virtuoso advisor with everything filled in.

  • 67 US luxury hotels with 13 months of lowest nightly rates and 1,500+ room types
  • 6,500+ cruise sailings across 11 lines, weekend getaways included
  • Quote requests that name the advisor amenities: breakfast, credits, upgrades, Virtuoso Voyages
  • Live filter counts so a number never promises more than the list shows
