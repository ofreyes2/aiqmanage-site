What it does

Draw, type, size and weld designs on a 12 by 12 or 12 by 24 mat, in inches or millimetres, then export true-size SVG, DXF or PNG for Silhouette Studio. Twelve bundled fonts plus runtime font install, image tracing, offset and weld, layers and undo.

  • DXF export that Silhouette Studio imports for free
  • Text to real cut paths in 12 fonts, install more at runtime
  • Trace a photo into a cuttable shape
  • AI copilot that drives the canvas with plain language
