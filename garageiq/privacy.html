Privacy Policy

GarageIQ collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Short version: GarageIQ has no account, no analytics, and no server. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Full stop.

What we store, and where

Everything you enter — your vehicle details, oil and service logs, checklist progress, reminders, and the emergency card — is saved only in local storage on your own device. It never leaves your phone and we never see it. Deleting the app removes it.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • No data sent to us or anyone else. The app works fully offline.

Notifications

If you choose to set a reminder, GarageIQ schedules a local notification on your device using iOS. These are created and delivered entirely on your phone; no reminder content is sent to any server.

Links to other sites

Some buttons open your browser to third-party sites (for example a YouTube search or the NHTSA recall lookup). Once you leave the app, those sites' own privacy policies apply. GarageIQ does not track which links you open.

Children

GarageIQ is a general utility rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
