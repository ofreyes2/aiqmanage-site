Getting started

Set up your vehicle

Open the Service tab and enter your year, make, model and engine, then tap Save. Your vehicle appears at the top of the app and tailors the how-to searches on the SOS tab to your exact car.

Track your oil

On the Oil tab, tap Log fill-up each time you get gas — every second one, GarageIQ reminds you to check the dipstick. When you add oil, log the date, odometer, and quarts. After two entries the chart shows your miles-per-quart trend, and a red banner appears if consumption crosses a level worth a mechanic's look.

Stay on top of service

Enter your current odometer on the Service tab. The "Due next" list then tracks oil changes, tire rotations, filters, and battery age. Record each service you do so the countdowns stay accurate.

Set a reminder

On the Service tab, tap a reminder button (for example "Check oil in 2 weeks"). GarageIQ asks permission to send notifications, then schedules a local alert on your phone. You can change notification permission any time in iOS Settings.

Using the tools

Decode a dash light

Open Decode → Dash lights and search the light you see. Each entry tells you red vs. amber urgency and exactly what to do now.

Look up an OBD code

Get a code from any $20 Bluetooth OBD-II reader, then type it into Decode → OBD codes for a plain-English cause. Most auto-parts stores also read codes for free.

Seasonal checklists

The Seasons tab highlights the current season and remembers what you've checked off. Switch tabs to plan ahead for another season.

Breakdown & emergency card

The SOS tab has a step-by-step breakdown playbook, jump-start instructions, and an emergency card you can fill in with your policy numbers and roadside contacts — all stored on your phone.

Common questions

Does GarageIQ work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your device with no account and no internet required. The only things that need a connection are the optional links that open your browser (YouTube, recall lookup).

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. See our Privacy Policy.

I moved to a new phone — can I transfer my logs?

Because everything is stored privately on-device, logs don't sync automatically. We're exploring an optional export/import; email us if that would help you.

Is GarageIQ a substitute for a mechanic?

No. It's general educational guidance for everyday owners. Your owner's manual and a trusted mechanic always win, and brake, fuel, airbag and suspension work belongs at a qualified shop.
